"One school. One idea. A new way of seeing design"

This website explores the history and ideas behind Bauhaus, from its beginnings in Germany in 1919 to its influence on architecture, furniture, typography, and visual art

Walter Gropius

Founded in Germany in 1919 by Walter Gropius, the Bauhaus brought together art, architecture, craft, and technology. Its goal was not simply to create beautiful objects, but to rethink how design could become part of everyday life.

The idea of bauhaus

The Bauhaus questioned the separation between art and function. Artists, architects, and designers experimented with geometry, colour, materials, typography, and space to create work that was both practical and expressive.
Although the school existed for only fourteen years, its ideas travelled far beyond Germany. Its influence can still be seen in architecture, furniture, graphic design, typography, and the visual world around us today.
